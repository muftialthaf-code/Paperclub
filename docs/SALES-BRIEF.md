# Paperclub for Cafés: Sales Brief

## What it is
Paperclub is a family games website that a café brands as its own. Guests scan one QR code, enter the café's six-digit code of the day, and play. There is nothing to download, no accounts to create and no hardware to buy.

## The pitch in one line
Give every table something to do besides their phones, so families and groups stay longer, order more and come back, and your café becomes the place that has the games.

## What guests get
- 24 screen-light party and family games: trivia, Would You Rather, Don't Say Yes or No, 5 Second Challenge, drawing games and more.
- Fully bilingual, English and Saudi Najdi Arabic, with a proper right-to-left layout.
- Difficulty adapts to who is playing (kids, mixed family, adults), so one table of mixed ages stays engaged.
- Works on any phone browser. Tens of thousands of prompts, so games rarely repeat.

## What the café gets
- **Your own branded page**: your logo centred at the top, colours picked automatically from your logo, and your name on the guest experience.
- **Your menu inside the games**: your dishes and drinks appear in trivia, Would You Rather and challenge rounds, so guests meet your menu while having fun.
- **One QR code per café**: print it on a table card. We provide a bilingual A5/A4 print card.
- **A six-digit code of the day** (24-hour window) that staff share at the table. It keeps play to real customers and rotates automatically if someone tries to guess it.
- **No play timer.** Guests play as long as they are there.
- **Multi-branch ready**: set the brand, layout and games once for the whole chain and let each branch inherit it, or customise per branch.
- **Reports**: scans, sessions and games played per branch and across the chain.
- **Staff roles**: owner, manager and staff, with branch-limited access.

## Why it is safe to buy
- Guests do not create accounts, so there is no personal data to manage.
- The platform owner reviews brand text before it goes public.
- Business logins support a second factor, and each business is isolated from the others.

## Who to target
Family-friendly cafés, shisha-free daytime cafés, restaurants with long waits, kids' venues, and chains that want one consistent experience across branches.

## Suggested conversation openers
1. "How do you keep a family table happy for the 20 minutes before food arrives?"
2. "What would it be worth if guests stayed one drink longer?"
3. Demo: scan, enter the code, and play one round with their own menu inside it. Two minutes is enough.

## Honest status (so the sales person does not over-promise)
- Pricing and billing are not built yet. Quote only what has been agreed in person.
- Arabic copy is awaiting native-speaker review.
- Impact on spend or dwell time is not yet measured. Do not quote numbers. Offer a pilot and share the report data.
- Menu items appear in some games (trivia, Would You Rather, 5 Second Challenge, drawing and spoken prompts), not all 26.
